## 1. Hiring expansion

Input: “We have 12 SaaS staff and plan to hire 23 more before renewal results arrive.”

Acceptable reply: Webvan disclosed facility volumes below profitability levels in its
[annual report filed April 2, 2001](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1092657/000101287001001485/0001012870-01-001485.txt).
That suggests a possible capacity-before-demand analogy, not a finding about your team.
SaaS hiring differs from grocery facilities in margins, lead times and cost flexibility.
Your paid backlog and renewal outlook are missing. One untested check is to compare the
new team's committed cost with contracted revenue under a weaker-renewal scenario you choose.
If firm orders cover that cost, the concern weakens; the analogy does not decide whether to hire.

Manual result: source beside fact, scope/difference and one falsifiable check present.
No withdrawn warehouse cost, city count or investor-motive claim needed.

## 2. Long lease

Input: “We want to sign a five-year lease while our customers renew monthly.”

Acceptable reply: WeWork's [draft S-1, confidentially submitted December 28, 2018](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1533523/000162827918000370/filename1.htm)
discussed longer landlord commitments and shorter member commitments. We checked those
sections, not the entire filing; its public-release date is not established here.
This raises a duration-mismatch question, not proof your lease is unsuitable. You may use
the office for your own business rather than rent desks onward, and reserves and break clauses
can change the comparison. One untested check: align unavoidable lease payments with contracted
receipts, including a customer-loss scenario you choose and documented exit rights.
Reliable coverage and an affordable exit would weaken the concern. Obtain qualified advice
for interpreting legal obligations; the Skill is not a substitute.

Manual result: source/date, confidential-draft caveat, difference and one check present.
No claim that the 2019 IPO decision and 2023 bankruptcy were the same event or inevitable sequence.

claim, pending-case recommendation or withdrawn allegation. Difference and one check present.
